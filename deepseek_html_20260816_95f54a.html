Our story

Investors Ultra · A legacy of trust

Investors Ultra was founded in Kampala, Uganda in 2021 with a single mission: to democratize access to premium technology investments for everyday Ugandans. What began as a small initiative with three laptops and a vision has grown into one of Uganda's most trusted digital investment platforms.


Our founders, Daniel Okello and Grace Nakato, recognized that many hardworking individuals wanted to participate in the growing tech economy but lacked the capital to purchase high-end equipment. By allowing investors to buy shares in premium laptops starting from just UGX 5,000, we opened the doors to a new world of financial opportunity.


Today, Investors Ultra manages a portfolio of over 2,500 premium machines and has paid out more than UGX 1.2 billion in returns to our investors. Our commitment to transparency, daily income distributions, and 24-hour withdrawal processing has earned us the trust of thousands across the country.
